THE GAME

YOUR CHOICES BUILD YOUR JOURNEY

You experience a situation, make a decision, take action, and live with the consequences. Then the wisdom behind the experience is revealed.

Experience → Choose → Act → Consequence → Discover → Grow

1

PLAY A JOURNEY

WISE has five Journeys with 12 missions in each one. Every mission gives you a real-life situation and a decision. Your choices can build Wisdom, Character, Courage and Self-Control.

2

DISCOVER THE WISDOM

After a mission, Wisdom Discovered reveals the Proverb connected to what happened. You are not trying to guess a Bible answer. You are discovering how Biblical wisdom can guide real choices.

3

MOVE TO THE NEXT JOURNEY

Complete all 12 missions to reach a Journey checkpoint. Your progress is saved and CrossTalk gives you a chance to hear from Maya, Jay, Leo and Zoe. Then the next Journey opens with new situations and challenges. Your scores, discoveries and rewards carry forward.

4

BUILD YOUR WISE COLLECTION

The WISE Collection is your reward shelf. Items unlock as you complete Journey checkpoints, so the Collection becomes a visual record of how far you have travelled. Journey 1 begins your Collection, Journey 2 completes the opening set and unlocks its own rewards, and later Journeys add their own items. Completing Journey 5 unlocks the 60 Proverbs Milestone.

5

GROW YOUR WISDOM VAULT

The Wisdom Vault stores every Proverb you discover, together with Scripture in Simple Words. Return to it whenever you want to remember a principle, reflect on a decision or use that wisdom in another situation.

6

USE MY QUIET SPACE

My Quiet Space becomes available after you complete a Journey. It gives you an optional place to pause before moving on—to write a prayer, record something you are thankful for or name an area where you need wisdom. Prayer does not earn points or rewards; it is simply a quiet space for reflection.

7

KEEP GROWING

WISE is about practising wisdom, not being perfect. Your decisions have consequences, but every Journey gives you another opportunity to think, learn and grow.
